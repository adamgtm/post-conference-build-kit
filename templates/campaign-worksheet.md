## Measurement

Keep these counts separate: collected people → qualified people → matched people → reached people → visitors → conversions.

For a newsletter offer, distinguish new subscribers, existing-subscriber claims, and repeat claims. Set a follow-up quality measure such as subsequent newsletter engagement.
