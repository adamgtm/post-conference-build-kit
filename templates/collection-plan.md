## Stopping rules

- Goal: what observed result will count as enough for this reader?
- Budget: next run cap must fit after spent charges, reservations, and any protected recap allowance.
- Weak-round threshold: record before collection. Suggested starting point: at most two newly discovered confirmed posts and no promising new lead. Adjust for the event's scale with a recorded reason, never to justify an early exit after the fact.
- Diminishing returns: use the skill's cross-route check; record supporting rounds and disposition of remaining leads.
- Pauses: record interruption or collection failure as unfinished work with a resumption step.
